Local crawl space experts you can trust

Charleston Crawlspace Co is a locally owned and operated company that does one thing: it fixes and protects the crawl spaces under Charleston homes. We are licensed and insured, and we answer our own phone. When you reach out, you are talking to the crew that does the work, not a sales center reading from a script and handing you off to a subcontractor you have never met.

We built the company around a simple idea. Crawl space work is invisible once it is finished. You will probably never see the liner, the seams or the sealed vents again, which means you are trusting the person who did the work. We would rather earn that trust than ask for it.

Why we started

Our background is in certified mold inspection and remediation across the Lowcountry. After enough jobs, the same pattern kept showing up. A homeowner would hire a company to encapsulate the crawl space, and the work would look fine on the day. Months later the smell would be back, the humidity would be high and mold would be growing again. When we got under the house, we would find the same causes: a liner laid over debris, vents left open, no way to drain water, no dehumidifier, or insulation left soaking above it all.

The problem was never the idea of encapsulation. It was the shortcuts. A system that is supposed to seal a space and control its moisture does not work if one of the pieces is missing. So we started a company that does the whole system, in the right order, with the cause of the moisture fixed first.

What we believe about crawl space work

Fix the cause, not the symptom. Mold on a joist is the result. Humidity, water or a leak is the cause. We solve the cause.

The order matters. Cleanout, repairs and drainage come before the liner. Sealing and drying come after. Doing things out of order is how jobs fail.

Honest scopes. If a smaller job will do, we will tell you. If you need drainage or repairs before anything else, we will show you why.

Show, do not tell. You see photographs of what we find. You see the finished space. You hear explanations in plain language.

Stand behind the work. We live in the same community as our customers. If something is not right, we want to hear about it, and we want to fix it.

What it is like to work with us

You call. Someone who works on crawl spaces answers. We ask about your neighborhood, your home and what you are seeing. We schedule an inspection, get under the house and take readings and photographs. We explain what we found, in words you can follow, and we give you a written scope and price. If you choose to move ahead, we do the work in the right order, keep you informed and walk you through the finished space.
