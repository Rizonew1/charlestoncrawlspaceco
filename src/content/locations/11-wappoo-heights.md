## Crawl space encapsulation in Wappoo Heights

Wappoo Heights is a West Ashley neighborhood near Wappoo Creek, close to the routes that connect West Ashley to James Island and the peninsula. It is a residential area of mid-century homes on established lots, with convenient access to the rest of the city.

Its location near tidal creeks and marsh shapes its crawl spaces. Groundwater here moves with the tides, summer humidity is intense and houses built decades ago were not designed for those conditions. We help owners seal, drain and dry their crawl spaces.

## What makes Wappoo Heights crawl spaces different

**Marsh and creek proximity.** Tidal water and marsh soils keep the ground wet and the air humid.

**Mid-century builds.** Most homes are from the 1950s and 1960s and use vented crawl spaces with minimal ground cover.

**Low elevation in parts of the neighborhood.** Some lots sit only slightly above tidal water.

**Salt exposure.** Salty air and brackish water speed up corrosion of metals in the crawl space.

**Storm exposure.** Hurricanes and strong storms bring surge and heavy rain that test every crawl space.

## Homes in Wappoo Heights

Typical homes are one-story ranches, brick or frame, with simple rectangular footprints. Many have carports, converted garages and rear additions. Interiors have been updated over the years, while crawl spaces often remain as built.

Foundations are generally masonry walls and piers with foundation vents. Ducts, supply lines and drains run through the crawl space, and many show signs of moisture damage.

## Water, soil and flood conditions in Wappoo Heights

**Tidal groundwater.** Water tables rise and fall with the tide and storms.

**Standing water.** Low sections of the neighborhood can hold water under houses.

**Humid, salty air.** Summer dew points in the 70s and salt in the air attack framing and metal.

**Storm surge risk.** Many properties are in mapped flood zones, and flooding can occur in major storms.

**Slow-draining soils.** Marsh-influenced soils drain poorly and stay wet.

## A closer look at Wappoo Heights

Wappoo Heights sits where West Ashley meets the creeks and marsh that separate it from James Island. Soils stay saturated longer, so liner and drainage design matters more. Humidity is high even by Charleston standards, so dehumidifier capacity needs a little extra margin. None of that makes encapsulation a bad idea here. It makes it an especially good one, provided it is designed for the conditions. A liner that is sealed, drained and conditioned does the job whether the creek is at low tide or high.

## What to plan for before storm season

Homeowners near tidal water should treat the crawl space as part of their storm plan. Before hurricane season, test the sump pump by pouring water into the basin and confirming it runs and discharges away from the house. Make sure the discharge line is open and not buried or crushed. Consider a battery backup. Check that your dehumidifier is running and draining. Look at the liner for tears or lifted seams. Know where your engineered flood vents are, if you have them, and leave them alone.

After a storm, look at the crawl space as soon as it is safe. Standing water, a pump that has stopped or new staining are all reasons to call us early. Water that sits for days causes much more damage than water that is removed quickly.

## A typical project in Wappoo Heights

A typical Wappoo Heights job starts with water. Even in houses that never see standing water, the soil is damp, the air is salty and the metal under the house is corroded. We begin by finding where moisture is coming from and how it is moving.

The result is a crawl space that does not depend on the weather to stay dry. It is steady in July, steady after a storm and steady through king tides.

## Keeping a Wappoo Heights crawl space healthy

Test the sump pump before every hurricane season. Keep the discharge line open and directed away from the house. Replace the dehumidifier filter on schedule. Look at metal components for rust, since salt air takes a toll. After storms, check the space as soon as it is safe, and call if you find standing water or a stopped pump.

## What salt air does to a crawl space

Salt air is easy to forget about until you look at the metal under a house. Duct straps rust through, galvanized hangers crumble, screws bleed rust onto wood and old pipe fittings corrode at the joints. In Wappoo Heights, we regularly replace hardware that failed years before the wood did. When we encapsulate a space, we specify corrosion-resistant fasteners and supports where it matters, and we seal the space so salt-laden air stops circulating through it. That protects both the structure and the equipment inside.
